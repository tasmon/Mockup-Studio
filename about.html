What is this app?

Mockup Studio is a Progressive Web App that lets you upload screenshots and instantly wrap them in realistic device frames. Perfect for app store listings, marketing materials, blog posts, social media, portfolios, and presentations.

What's new in v2.5.0

  • 🆕 Image fit modes: cover, contain, fill, none
  • 🆕 Zoom in/out with slider and quick buttons
  • 🆕 Drag-to-position your image
  • 🆕 Transparent background support (PNG/WebP)
  • 🆕 Custom background color picker
  • ✅ 8 device types (iPhone, iPad, Android, Apple Watch, MacBook, Laptop, iMac, Smart TV)
  • ✅ Batch export multiple screenshots at once
  • ✅ Export as PNG, JPEG, or WebP
  • ✅ 8 color themes + custom accent color
  • ✅ Works offline (installable PWA)

Privacy

Your images never leave your device. Everything is processed locally in your browser. No servers, no uploads, no analytics. The app works offline once installed.

Open Source & Free

This is a free, open project. Use it however you like. Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no heavy frameworks.
